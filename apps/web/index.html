Fingerpost talks your finger to the right button on any touchscreen.

A free Android app for blind and low-vision people. Point your phone at a kiosk, ticket machine or appliance and say what you want to press. It guides you with speech, a rising tone and vibration, then checks the press worked.

Four steps from “where is it?” to “done”.

The phone does the looking. You keep your hand on the screen and listen.

  1. 1

    Point

    Hold the phone toward the screen. It tells you when the whole screen is in view.

  2. 2

    Say

    “Medium drink.” “Start the wash.” “Pay by card.” Plain words are enough.

  3. 3

    Follow

    “Left. Up a little.” The tone rises as you get closer, and the phone buzzes on target.

  4. 4

    Press

    Tap, and Fingerpost looks again to check the screen changed the way you meant.

Three ways to know where your finger is.

Voice

Short directions: “left”, “up a little”, “press now”. Spoken through TalkBack’s voice or the phone’s own.

Tone

The pitch climbs as you close in, so you can hear the distance without waiting for words.

Vibration

A firm buzz when you are on target. A vibration-only mode for deafblind users is planned.

It speaks your language.

Guidance, questions and confirmations in English, Hindi and Spanish. Choose the language in settings, or let it follow your phone.

Private where it matters most.

Before any picture leaves the phone, Fingerpost checks it for a keypad or PIN wording. When it finds one, it stops uploading and turns the microphone off until the keypad is gone.

It can tell you which digit is under your finger, but only through earphones. It never asks for, sends, logs or stores your PIN.

“PIN pad detected. I'll stay offline while you enter it.”

What Fingerpost says when it sees a PIN pad
  • You approve every payment. Before any pay or confirm button, it reads the amount and waits for your spoken “yes”.
  • Pictures are not kept. Screens and your voice are used to find buttons, then thrown away. Faces are blurred on the phone first.
  • No ads, no tracking. No third-party analytics, and no account with your name.

Questions

Is Fingerpost free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the Apache-2.0 licence, with no ads.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones running Android 10 or newer, with a rear camera. There is no iPhone version yet.

Do I need TalkBack?

No. Fingerpost speaks for itself, and it also works with TalkBack turned on.

What happens without internet?

After a few seconds without a connection it says “Basic mode: text buttons only.” It keeps guiding to buttons with readable text, using the phone alone, and nothing is uploaded.

Will it press anything for me?

No. You always press. Before a pay or confirm button it reads the amount and waits for your “yes”.

Can I use it on medical devices?

No. Fingerpost is an assistive aid. Double-check important actions, and do not use it for medical devices.
