Find the right button on any touchscreen, without sight.

Fingerpost is an Android app in development for blind and low-vision people. Point your phone at a flat touchscreen and say what you want to press. We're testing guidance with speech, a rising tone and vibration, followed by a check that the press worked.

Who it's for

Blind and low-vision people who meet flat touchscreens with no buttons to feel: self-order kiosks, ticket machines, check-in screens and home appliances.

What we're building

  • Guides one finger to one button, or walks you through a whole order, step by step.
  • Reads the amount and waits for your spoken “yes” before any pay button.
  • Never sends your PIN anywhere. On a PIN pad, the camera stays on your phone and digits are spoken only into your earphones.
  • Free and open source. Works with TalkBack. English, Hindi and Spanish.

Check GitHub for signed APK releases. Release availability and device acceptance are still pending; read the install guide first.
