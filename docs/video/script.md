# Three-minute demo script

Draft for C6-2, following design §8. Capture and measured results are pending. Bracketed directions are production notes, not claims to narrate. Obtain consent before recording anyone; describe proxy trials as proxies. Never capture actual PINs, personal screens, recognized content in logs or credentials.

| Time | Picture and narration |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | [Verified appliance take.] “A flat touchscreen has no buttons to feel. Fingerpost is being built to help you find the control with speech, tone and vibration.” [If device acceptance is unavailable, label the take a development demonstration.] |
| 0:12–0:30 | [Close-up of the flat panel and the phone.] “Appliances and kiosks often leave blind people dependent on someone else. We want the phone to give practical guidance and check whether the action worked.” |
| 0:30–1:25 | [Tablet simulator, wide shot showing phone and pointing hand.] “Order a medium cola.” [Record the actual question, answer, target guidance and price confirmation. Show PIN mode only with a synthetic keypad and earphones.] “Before payment, the amount needs confirmation. PIN assistance stays on the phone, with microphone and uploads stopped.” [Display numeric no-upload evidence only after it is verified.] |
| 1:25–2:05 | [Architecture overlay.] “Voice Transcribe turns speech into a goal. Spark reads the screen and plans the steps. SAM refines the target. The phone tracks the finger and gives cues locally.” [Private-mode segment only after real Glimmer acceptance; otherwise label it implemented, deployment pending. Show measured quality and latency with sample count and revision; omit unavailable values.] |
| 2:05–2:35 | [Explore, repeated-screen visit and Hindi take.] “Explore describes the controls. Saved layouts can help with a familiar screen. Guidance includes Hindi and Spanish.” [Use actual captures; do not imply an unmeasured instant re-lock or accepted device phrasing.] |
| 2:35–3:00 | [Accessible install guide, source URL and release status.] “Fingerpost is free and open source. Follow development and help test it at fingerpost.web.app.” [Say “download the beta” only after safety gates and signed-release installation pass. Include consented quotes only when available.] |

Use captions for all speech, describe important visual changes in narration, and keep demonstration status visible. Cut optional clips before compressing the payment/PIN explanation. No demographic, legal or incident statistics are included without separately verified sources.
