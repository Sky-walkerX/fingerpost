## English email

Subject: Optional Fingerpost Android accessibility practice session

Hello,

We're building Fingerpost for the Meta hackathon. It is an unfinished Android prototype intended to guide a blind or low-vision person's finger to touchscreen controls using speech, tones and vibration. Camera tracking and a tone prototype work, but tracking speed and the complete guidance loop still need development.

Would your organization consider sharing an invitation or advising us on an accessible future session? We propose an optional slot of up to 45 minutes, in person or by video call using the participant's Android phone. It uses practice screens and non-payment appliance controls; no real payment or real PIN is involved.

Participation is voluntary. Saved measurements, notes, quotations and name credit each need separate permission. English and Hindi consent information is available, including an option to have it read aloud.

Please reply to this email with interest or questions. No participant names, medical information or phone details are needed at this stage. A human facilitator will confirm readiness and arrangements privately.

Thank you.

## English Reddit draft

Title: Feedback invitation for an unfinished Android touchscreen-guidance prototype

We're building Fingerpost for the Meta hackathon. The aim is to help blind and low-vision Android users find touchscreen controls through speech, tones and vibration. We have camera tracking and a tone prototype. Tracking performance still needs work, and the complete guidance loop is not ready.

We're interested in future, voluntary practice sessions lasting up to 45 minutes, using practice screens and non-payment appliance controls. No real payment, real PIN or private account is involved. Participants could stop or skip any task. English and Hindi consent information can be read aloud.

Saving task measurements, written notes, approved quotations or name credit would each require separate permission. We would not record camera images, audio, video or private screen text. Please do not post personal, medical or device-identifying information in comments.

This is a hackathon prototype, not an established accessibility product. A human facilitator would explain the current limitations and confirm readiness and a private contact route before a session. We will only share this invitation where community rules permit it.
